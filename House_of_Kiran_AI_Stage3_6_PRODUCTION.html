Advanced rules
Stacking: 2 same-colour goti can stack anywhere. A stack moves only on even rolls (2→1, 4→2, 6→3 steps). Unstacking only at any colour's start square or a star square.

Blocking: nobody — not even the owner — can move past a 2-stack.

Killing: only stack-vs-stack kills. A single can never kill or be killed by a stack. An opponent single can perch on top of a 2-stack (frozen there) — a second goti of that same colour completes their stack and kills the one underneath.

Sixes: a 6 grants an extra roll. Three 6s in a row cancels that run of moves, then you get one more roll.

Kills: capturing grants a bonus roll — the killer goti can't move again until your next turn, but everything else stays usable, including any of this turn's dice you haven't spent yet.
